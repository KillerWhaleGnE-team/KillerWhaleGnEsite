Services

A few ways we help other creators — and ourselves — stay online without the headache.

Web Hosting Without the Headache

Already built your website or web game, but don't want to spend hours on Cloudflare, databases, deployment, and everything in between? Send us your project — we'll handle the rest.

We provide customizable hosting and deployment for independent developers, creators, and small projects — taking your work from a folder of files to a working, publicly accessible service. Depending on what you need, we can help with deployment, debugging, optimization, hosting, databases, backend services, and custom infrastructure. Simple projects may cost little or nothing; larger projects are priced by resource and database needs.

Privacy when you need it: by default we credit projects under your preferred name or alias, no personal email required. For projects that need extra privacy, we can host without publicly linking your identity or contact info to the project — useful for personal projects, experimental games, private commissions, or just keeping your inbox clear.

Free – under $1, depending on project

Quick & Short Server Hosting

Need a game server for a few days, not a few months? We set up short-term hosting (e.g. via AxentHost) on request — pay a small fee and get a server running immediately with the specs you want, no waiting to earn credits over time.
